# Trial exit gate

Development does not pass this gate. Only real practitioner evidence may pass it.

Proceed toward the next engine milestone only after at least three independent experienced planners complete the full workflow; five is preferable. No unresolved finding may demonstrate a fundamental defect in trusted historical state, remaining-work semantics, recovery executability, realistic planner information requirements, deterministic persistence, or material result comprehensibility.

Participants should distinguish accepted actual history, the status point, remaining work, the future recovery, and the approved plan. They should not routinely invent information outside normal planning practice. They should understand material movement well enough to decide whether to investigate or accept the result.

Classify findings:

- **A — Gate Blocker:** fundamental scheduling, trusted-state, persistence, or model problem.
- **B — Important Usability Issue:** semantics appear sound but UI/workflow materially prevents valid use.
- **C — Later Improvement:** convenience, terminology, presentation, or polish that does not prevent the trial outcome.

The gate does not require a perfect UI, unanimous preference, production readiness, or zero usability complaints.
