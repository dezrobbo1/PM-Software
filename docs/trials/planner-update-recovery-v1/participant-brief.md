# Participant brief

## Introduction

PM-Software is experimental research into a different approach to professional scheduling. It is not a finished commercial product. This trial tests update and recovery after execution has begun and should take about 25 minutes.

Please work as you normally would. We want to learn where the approach feels natural, confusing, incomplete, or untrustworthy. Candid criticism is useful; there is no requirement to agree with the system.

## Your outcome

Review the approved starting plan and the field update. Establish the accepted status point, record what actually happened and what remains, calculate a recovery, inspect the material change, and approve it only if you are satisfied. Save the workspace, reopen it in a fresh browser context, and confirm the trusted history and recovered approval remain.

Do not redesign the starting network. This trial is about update and recovery.

## Need help?

Relative time uses `day@time`, such as `1@12:00`. Every activity needs an explicit status. A status update becomes trusted only after acceptance. Calculation creates a proposal; approval remains a separate action.
