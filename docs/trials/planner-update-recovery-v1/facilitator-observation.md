Observe and note:

- Hesitation and requests for explanation.
- Terminology misunderstood.
- Whether actual history, remaining work, future forecast, status point, and approval are distinguished.
- Attempts to edit the wrong layer.
- Information expected to already exist, or invented/looked up.
- Understanding of resource quantities and group-level identity.
- Understanding of what moved, what stayed fixed, and why.
- Any result distrusted and the stated reason.
- Whether save/reopen is understood and completed.

Classify each finding as a product/model issue or a UI/workflow issue, then assign Gate Blocker, Important Usability Issue, or Later Improvement using the exit gate.
