# Completion questionnaire

1. What did you think the system was asking you to do?
2. Was the distinction between what had actually happened, what remained to be done, and the future recovered plan clear?
3. Could you understand the important reasons that work moved in the recovered schedule?
4. Did the system ask for information that you would not normally know or maintain as a planner/scheduler?
5. Would you trust the recovered result enough to investigate or use it in real planning work? Why or why not?
6. What was the most confusing or unnatural part of the workflow?
7. What, if anything, did the system do better than your normal update process?

The UI can download these responses together with the current trial result. It does not submit or persist feedback.
