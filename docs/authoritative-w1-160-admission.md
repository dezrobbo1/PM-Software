## Authority and bounds

The public scheduler materializes all original locally executable mode placements and records each activity's original one based rank before pruning. For each of at most 16 authorised structures, W1 repeatedly discards rows without necessary finish/start precedence support and unions surviving original ranks across structures. W1 ignores shared resource and workface contention; any actual schedule survives its necessary support tests. A retained original rank, including holes in the rank sequence, remains its canonical digit coefficient. The digit maximum stays the original eligible domain length. Timing weights use original declared activity indices. The original LB1 finish proof, shared 60 deterministic unit finish budget, exact weighted timing objective, safe mixed radix blocks and independent physical validation remain in force. UNKNOWN never becomes infeasibility or an optimum.

The ordering of bounds is: 160 declared activities and 16 structures at validation; 100,000 raw placement rows during preprocessing; at most 20,000 **retained** placement literals and 20,000 workface intervals before CP-SAT construction. Horizon stays at 480 ticks, per stage deterministic budgets stay at 60, and the canonical integer safety bound is unchanged. The raw guard is checked inside placement generation, before constructing the first row beyond the remaining allowance, including when a single mode would cross it. This is an explicit bounded construction profile: the retained faithful professional input generates 64,068 raw placements. This experiment does not certify arbitrary 160 activity inputs with larger raw domains. The 20,000 placement admission is now applied to the actual solver literals, not to rows safely removed by W1.

Historical sequential and batched plans keep their original compiler identities, hashes and bytes. Read only validation needs no solver calls. New plans identify `native-work-method-time-w1-lb1-seeded/0`; their hashes naturally change because proof identity changes while the plan schema, policy and hash algorithm remain unchanged. Historical objective and canonical oracles use the unpruned compiler explicitly, rather than claiming to reproduce a newly authoritative model.

Accepted history and rolling recovery retain their earlier **64 declared activity** scope. They may validate old plans and cycles without recalculation. The general future only scheduler admits 65 through 160 within its bounded domain, but this does not silently expand accepted history or rolling recovery to professional 160 scale.
